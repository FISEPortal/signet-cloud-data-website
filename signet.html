Core Architecture

Three primitives. One proof.

Signet governs every request against a person's neurodata the same way, regardless of who or what is asking.

IDENTITY

User-controlled keys

Automated cryptographic keys, portable via W3C Decentralized Identifiers, with recovery built in from day one: proof of who, or what, is requesting access.

POLICY

Per-event consent

Purpose-specific consent enforced per data-processing event, not agreed to once and forgotten. Every grant is scoped, time-bound, and revocable at the infrastructure layer.

PROOF

Auditable compliance

A cryptographically auditable log chain aligned to ISO/IEC TS 27571:2026 and TIPPSS: compliance an enterprise can hand to a regulator or a court.

What's Next

Today, the parties requesting access are human: researchers, scientists, biobanks. Autonomous systems are next, and the architecture doesn't have to change to accommodate them. The same keys. The same per-event policy. The same proof.
